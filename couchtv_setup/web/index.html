Couch TV Setup

    Put Couch TV on your Fire TV Stick

    A clean home screen with no adverts, your apps up front, the time and the weather. About ten minutes, and you can undo all of it.

    • Your Fire TV Stick, switched on, with its remote.
    • This computer on the same Wi-Fi as the TV.

    This computer is only needed today. Once it is set up, Couch TV runs on the TV by itself.

    Switch on ADB debugging

    This lets this computer make the changes on the TV. Use the TV remote:

    1. Open Settings (the gear at the right of the top row), then My Fire TV.
    2. If you see Developer options, skip to step 4.
    3. Open About and press the select button on your stick's name seven times. "You are now a developer" shows. Press Back.
    4. Open Developer options and switch ADB debugging on. Say OK to the warning.

    Find your TV

    Looking on your Wi-Fi…

    Type the address instead

    On the TV: Settings, My Fire TV, About, Network. Type the IP Address it shows.

    Nothing yet? Check the TV is on and on the same Wi-Fi as this computer, and that ADB debugging is on. If the Mac asked to "find devices on your local network", it needs Allow (System Settings, Privacy & Security, Local Network). If Windows asked whether to allow Couch TV Setup on networks, it needs Allow. A guest or "Public" Wi-Fi can keep devices apart: use your home Wi-Fi.

    Install Couch TV

    This TV already has a Couch TV app that was built by someone else, so it cannot be updated in place. Replacing it is safe, but its settings (apps order, weather) start fresh.

    Make it your home screen

    Setting Couch TV as the home screen. The TV may flash for a moment.

    One last thing: Fire OS has not started Couch TV's Home watcher yet. A restart starts it (this happens after some updates). It takes about a minute, and this page carries on by itself.

    One press on the TV: Couch TV's screen is open on your TV. With the remote, highlight Use as home screen and press the select button.

    Try it

    This presses Home on the TV for you. Couch TV should come up.

    Switch off Amazon's adverts and tracking?

    Optional. These Amazon parts only show adverts, track what you watch or nag. They are switched off, not deleted, and the Undo button switches them back on.

      All done 🎉

      • Move apps around: hold the select button on an app.
      • More settings: the gear tile at the end of your apps.
      • Amazon's home flashes up for a moment when you press Home. That is normal on a Fire TV.
      • After a Couch TV update, if Home goes to Amazon's home, restart the TV once.
      • To undo: open the Couch TV app on the TV and choose Stop being the home screen.

      You can switch ADB debugging off again now (Settings, My Fire TV, Developer options). Switch it back on if you ever run this setup again.
